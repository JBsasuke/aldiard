Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

At Al Día RD (aldiard.com) we respect your privacy. This page explains what information is collected when you visit the site and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to register and we do not store your name, email or other personal data while you browse. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Cookies and advertising

This site may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookie (DoubleClick) enables Google and its partners to show you ads based on your browsing across the internet.

You can turn off personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party cookies at aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc.) and uses Google Fonts. These services may log basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to serve and protect the site, according to their own privacy policies.

Some stories include YouTube videos, loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). If you play a video, YouTube may use cookies according to its own policy.

We store in your browser, only on your device, whether you prefer light or dark mode and your language (Spanish or English). This information is not sent anywhere.

Links to other sites

Our stories link to original sources and other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
