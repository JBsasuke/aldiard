Government Announces Christmas Bonus Payment and Public Investment Boost

The Dominican government will pay over RD$35 billion in Christmas bonuses starting December 3 and raise public investment.

Key points

  • The Christmas bonus payment starts Thursday, December 3, totaling RD$35,619,302,872.
  • The payment covers public servants, pensioners, police officers, and military personnel.
  • Public investment would rise to RD$230,000 million this year, according to Finance and Economy Minister Magín Díaz.
  • The 2026 Christmas customs allowance for Dominicans abroad remains at US$5,500.

The Dominican government announced a series of measures ahead of the Christmas season, including the Christmas bonus payment, which begins Thursday, December 3. According to Félix Reyna, director of Diecom, the total disbursement amounts to RD$35,619,302,872. The payment covers public servants, pensioners, police officers, and military personnel.

The Directorate General of Customs will also keep the 2026 Christmas customs allowance at US$5,500 for Dominicans living abroad. The benefit lets travelers bring gifts, personal effects, and household items duty-free, as long as they do not exceed that value or amount to commercial quantities. It applies to those arriving by air or sea, is granted per family, and requires family members to have spent at least six months without visiting the Dominican Republic.

On the economic front, Finance and Economy Minister Magín Díaz said the government is working on a budget modification to raise public investment to RD$230,000 million this year, aiming to keep the economy dynamic.

In health, the government delivered the Dr. Fidel Mejía Ramírez Regional Trauma Hospital in Yaguate, San Cristóbal. With this center, the National Trauma Network reaches six facilities, strengthening care in the southern region and reducing the need to transfer patients to Greater Santo Domingo.

During the week, President Luis Abinader reiterated that the government will keep acting against any corruption and called on citizens to report irregularities. In that context, the country was selected to host the XII Regional Anti-Corruption Conference, scheduled for December 1 to 4 in Punta Cana, with more than 2,000 leaders from 140 countries.

Why it matters

Public employees, pensioners, police, and military families will receive this payment before Christmas. Dominicans visiting the country for the holidays can bring gifts duty-free up to US$5,500 per family.
