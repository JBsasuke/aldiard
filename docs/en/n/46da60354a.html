Miami HEAT and Dominican Republic Team Up to Promote Tourism

The NBA champions are partnering with the Dominican Ministry of Tourism to attract travelers from Florida.

Key points

  • The partnership covers the 2026–2027 season and targets the Florida market.
  • The Ministry of Tourism, through the Dominican Republic tourism brand, is signing the deal with Miami HEAT.
  • The country projects it will close 2026 with more than 12 million visitors.
  • Miami HEAT is an NBA team with three championships.

The Dominican Republic's Ministry of Tourism, through the Dominican Republic tourism brand, and the Miami HEAT announced a partnership for the 2026–2027 season. The goal is to connect basketball fans with an interest in visiting the Dominican destination. Miami HEAT, which has won three NBA championships, is one of the league's most recognized teams.

The collaboration carries special weight in Florida, one of the states that sends the most tourists to the Dominican Republic. Through the deal, the country hopes to reach fans in that market and build a relationship with potential travelers through their shared passion for basketball.

The announcement comes at a time of growth for Dominican tourism, which projects more than 12 million visitors by the close of 2026. In that context, the country continues expanding its international promotion to showcase what it offers.

David Collado, Dominican Minister of Tourism, said that "sport connects people and allows us to bring the Dominican Republic to new audiences." According to the minister, the partnership aims to turn the excitement of basketball into a desire to discover the country, and he highlighted Florida's importance as a tourism market.

For its part, John Vidalin, Executive Vice President and Chief Commercial Officer of Miami HEAT, said the team wants to share the country's culture and experiences with its fans. Vidalin said the partnership will create bonds that go beyond the court.

With this collaboration, the Dominican Republic aims to spark interest in its beaches, nature, gastronomy, and cultural expressions, integrating sports into its tourism promotion strategy.

Why it matters

For Dominicans in the U.S. and at home, the campaign aims to strengthen tourism, one of the country's main sources of income.
