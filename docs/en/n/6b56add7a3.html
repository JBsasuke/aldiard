Consuelo and Quisqueya Take Early Leads in 44th TBS Semifinals in San Pedro de Macorís

Consuelo and Quisqueya won the opening games of their semifinal series at the 44th TBS tournament in San Pedro de Macorís. Darlyn Thomas posted a double-double of 29 points and 11 rebounds.

Key points

  • Consuelo beat Centro 109-91 in Series A, best-of-five.
  • Quisqueya defeated Miramar's Titanes 76-74 in Series B.
  • Darlyn Thomas scored 29 points and grabbed 11 rebounds in Consuelo's win.
  • Elvis Bobadilla added 23 points for Quisqueya.

The Todos Estrellas de Consuelo and Correcaminos de Quisqueya won the first games of their semifinal series at the 44th Torneo de Baloncesto Superior de San Pedro de Macorís (TBS de SPM 2026), where the winner gets a chance at the BanReservas Cup.

In Series A, a best-of-five, Consuelo beat the Centro club 109-91. Native player Darlyn Thomas led the offense with 29 points and 11 rebounds. Erick Paulino added 24 points, and Wilbert Ribera had 17 points and eight rebounds. For Centro, Johan Donastorg scored 32 points and pulled down eight rebounds.

In Series B, also best-of-five, Quisqueya edged the Titanes de Miramar 76-74. U22 player Elvis Bobadilla scored 23 points and dished out seven assists, and national reinforcement Diego Colón from Azua added 20 points. For Miramar, Edinson Montilla recorded 19 points and 13 rebounds, and Wilson Valdez scored 18 points.

The games are being played at the Rolando Ramírez gymnasium, part of the Tomasito Binet Sports Complex in San Pedro de Macorís. The tournament is organized by the San Pedro de Macorís Basketball Association (Abasapema), led by Fausto Paulino.

The semifinals continue Monday the 23rd. At 7:00 p.m., Miramar and Quisqueya face off in Series B, and at 9:00 p.m., Centro and Consuelo play in Series A. If a fifth game is needed, Series A will be played Thursday the 26th at 7:00 p.m., and Series B at 9:00 p.m.
