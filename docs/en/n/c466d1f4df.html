Mariana Távarez Visits Plantain Processing Project in La Vega

The head of MERCADOM toured an aggregation and packing center in La Vega, where a plan to turn plantains into higher-value products is moving forward.

Key points

  • The visit took place on Friday, November 13, at the Mercado de Productores in La Vega.
  • Mariana Távarez and Operations Director Robinson Cuevas attended, and the event was presided over by President Luis Abinader.
  • The CAPLATAGRO La Vega project aims to extend the shelf life of plantains and add value to the harvest.
  • Stated goals include creating direct and indirect jobs and providing more price stability for consumers.

Mariana Távarez, general administrator of Mercados Dominicanos de Abasto Agropecuario (MERCADOM), and Operations Director Robinson Cuevas visited the Aggregation and Packing Center for Agricultural Products of COOPEVIVE, located in the Producers' Market in La Vega. The visit took place on Friday, November 13, and was presided over by President Luis Abinader Corona.

During the tour, the officials observed how the center operates and how its facilities are used for aggregating and packing agricultural products. There, COOPEVIVE, the Dominican Association of Plantain Producers (ADOPLATANO) and Randy Beato Export work together to make better use of the space and bring technology into their processes.

The event included the master plan for the Plantain Aggregation and Agro-industrial Processing Center (CAPLATAGRO La Vega). The proposal aims to extend the shelf life of plantains and add value to the harvest through processing. Its promoters cite as goals the creation of direct and indirect jobs, greater price stability for consumers, and a contribution to the country's food security.

According to MERCADOM, the visit allowed it to see firsthand the work of producers and their organizations before food reaches the market. Aggregation, packing and processing affect the conditions under which products are sold and the opportunities producers have to place their harvests.

As the administering institution of Merca Santo Domingo, MERCADOM maintains contact with cooperatives and producer associations to understand their needs and assess how initiatives like CAPLATAGRO La Vega can strengthen the marketing of national production.

Why it matters

If the project advances, it could create jobs in plantain-producing areas and help stabilize plantain prices for Dominican families.
