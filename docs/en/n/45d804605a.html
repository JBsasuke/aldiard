Chey Lashes Out at Ramón Tolentino and Admits He Doesn't Know What Will Happen to His Marriage

Chey hits back at Ramón Tolentino in a televised interview, denies paying to cover up the Melissa Medina case, and admits he doesn't know if he'll stay married.

Key points

  • Chey criticized Ramón Tolentino over how, in his view, he has handled the Melissa Medina case.
  • He denied paying to cover up the case and said he is willing to be questioned by the Public Ministry.
  • His wife traveled to the United States, and he admitted he doesn't know what will happen to his marriage.

Eliezer Salvador Matos, known as Chey, lashed out at broadcaster Ramón Tolentino during an interview with businessman Santiago Matías on the reality show La Casa de Alofoke 3, according to De Último Minuto. In the conversation he also spoke about the death of Melissa Medina and his personal situation.

Chey questioned the public accusations made against him and said a negative image has been built of him that, in his words, does not match his behavior. He maintained he had no reason to harm Medina.

About Tolentino, Chey questioned how the broadcaster has handled the case and said he is not the monster, as he sees it, that has been presented to the public. He also objected to calls for him to appear before authorities, arguing that a broadcaster has no power to issue court summonses. According to the source, Tolentino did not offer a response to these statements in the available material.

Chey rejected reports of alleged protection and the use of money to avoid legal consequences. He denied making any payments for that purpose and said he wants all information about the case to come out so authorities can determine what happened. These claims have not been independently verified.

On his family, Chey said his wife traveled to the United States, where he said she has business commitments, and acknowledged: "I don't know what's going to happen" with his marriage. Although he said he remains in contact with her, he gave no further details.
